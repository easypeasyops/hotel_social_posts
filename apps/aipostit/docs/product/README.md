# Product notes

aipostit's product memory: what we are building, for whom, what we decided and why, and
which ideas wait. The aipostit-product-manager agent (`.claude/agents/aipostit-product-manager.md`,
available to sessions started at the repo root) reads these first and keeps them up to date;
anyone else can too. `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `RUNBOOK.md`
say how the product works today; these say where it is going.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [strategy.md](strategy.md) | The customer, the problem, the stage we're at, the constraints, how we decide |
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | Product decisions with their date and reason, newest first |
| [ideas.md](ideas.md) | Every idea with its status (now, next, parked, dropped) and the open questions |
| `spec-*.md` | One-page specs with their PR stack, e.g. [staff invite tracking](spec-staff-invite-tracking.md) |

Keep them short and true: change a line when a fact changes, rather than adding a new one
beside it. A guess is written as a guess until a customer confirms it.
